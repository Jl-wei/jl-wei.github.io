Jialiang Wei, postdoctoral fellow at Hasso Plattner Institute

I try to turn “this app is trash” into requirements a team can build.

I work in Prof. Dr. Walid Maalej's group at Hasso Plattner Institute. My research explores how AI can help close the communication gap between the people who use software and the people who build it, so that both sides end up describing the same product.

Research

Four topics, each introduced by the kind of feedback that motivates it.

What users writeWhat I work on

  • ★★★★★“Nice app, but something feels off.”

    Requirements engineering

    Working out what the software should do instead, precisely enough to build it.

  • ★★★★★“Why is the button over there?!”

    User interface

    Using AI to help search for and design better app screens.

  • ★★★★★“One star. Would give zero.”

    User feedback

    Processing feedback automatically, and helping users suggest fixes instead of complaining.

  • ★★★★★“I’m switching to that other app.”

    Mining software repositories

    Learning from what similar apps in the app stores already offer.
